You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 3 carts.

The carts are: NILA, PUGU, LATU. Books move NILA -> PUGU -> LATU.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "mumo" carries each NILA tray to a PUGU tray.
- "sito" is an internal rearrangement of PUGU: it carries each PUGU tray to a PUGU tray.
- "pisi" carries each PUGU tray to a LATU tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "mumo" and then "sito" lands on the same tray as running "mumo" alone.
     ("sito" leaves every tray that "mumo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "sito" and then "pisi" gives the same result as running "pisi" alone.
     ("pisi" cannot tell whether "sito" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "sito" three times in a row leaves every PUGU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- NILA has exactly one tray, called n1.
- LATU has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle cart (PUGU):
D1.1 PUGU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "sito" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one PUGU tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle cart.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

PUGU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
mumo: n1 -> <PUGU tray>
sito: q1_1 -> <PUGU tray>, ..., q1_k -> <PUGU tray>
pisi: q1_1 -> <LATU tray>, ... (one entry per PUGU tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
