You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 6 carts.

The carts are: ROSA, ZUZA, RUKO, LAKU, JUBU, POGU. Books move ROSA -> ZUZA -> RUKO -> LAKU -> JUBU -> POGU.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "niso" carries each ROSA tray to a ZUZA tray.
- "koji" is an internal rearrangement of ZUZA: it carries each ZUZA tray to a ZUZA tray.
- "kazu" carries each ZUZA tray to a RUKO tray.
- "bani" is an internal rearrangement of RUKO: it carries each RUKO tray to a RUKO tray.
- "kugu" carries each RUKO tray to a LAKU tray.
- "tivu" is an internal rearrangement of LAKU: it carries each LAKU tray to a LAKU tray.
- "sopi" carries each LAKU tray to a JUBU tray.
- "biza" is an internal rearrangement of JUBU: it carries each JUBU tray to a JUBU tray.
- "kudo" carries each JUBU tray to a POGU tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "niso" and then "koji" lands on the same tray as running "niso" alone.
     ("koji" leaves every tray that "niso" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "koji" and then "kazu" gives the same result as running "kazu" alone.
     ("kazu" cannot tell whether "koji" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "koji" twice in a row leaves every ZUZA tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "kazu" and then "bani" lands on the same tray as running "kazu" alone.
     ("bani" leaves every tray that "kazu" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "bani" and then "kugu" gives the same result as running "kugu" alone.
     ("kugu" cannot tell whether "bani" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "bani" twice in a row leaves every RUKO tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "kugu" and then "tivu" lands on the same tray as running "kugu" alone.
     ("tivu" leaves every tray that "kugu" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "tivu" and then "sopi" gives the same result as running "sopi" alone.
     ("sopi" cannot tell whether "tivu" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "tivu" twice in a row leaves every LAKU tray exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "sopi" and then "biza" lands on the same tray as running "sopi" alone.
     ("biza" leaves every tray that "sopi" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "biza" and then "kudo" gives the same result as running "kudo" alone.
     ("kudo" cannot tell whether "biza" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "biza" twice in a row leaves every JUBU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- ROSA has exactly one tray, called n1.
- POGU has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (ZUZA, RUKO, LAKU, JUBU):
D1.1 ZUZA must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D1.2 "koji" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one ZUZA tray).
D2.1 RUKO must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D2.2 "bani" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one RUKO tray).
D3.1 LAKU must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D3.2 "tivu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one LAKU tray).
D4.1 JUBU must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D4.2 "biza" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one JUBU tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).
