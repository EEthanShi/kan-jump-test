You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 3 carts.

The carts are: VUBI, LOBU, KUKA. Books move VUBI -> LOBU -> KUKA.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "zuja" carries each VUBI tray to a LOBU tray.
- "josi" is an internal rearrangement of LOBU: it carries each LOBU tray to a LOBU tray.
- "nasa" carries each LOBU tray to a KUKA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "zuja" and then "josi" lands on the same tray as running "zuja" alone.
     ("josi" leaves every tray that "zuja" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "josi" and then "nasa" gives the same result as running "nasa" alone.
     ("nasa" cannot tell whether "josi" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "josi" twice in a row leaves every LOBU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- VUBI has exactly 5 trays, called n1, n2, n3, n4, n5.
- KUKA has exactly 1 tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1, n2 to s1, n3 to s1, n4 to s1, n5 to s1.

Requirements for the middle cart (LOBU):
D1.1 LOBU must be a finite set with at most 7 trays.
D1.2 "josi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one LOBU tray).
D1.3 "zuja" must carry different VUBI trays to different LOBU trays.
D1.4 Every KUKA tray must be reached by "nasa" from at least one LOBU tray.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle cart.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 7 trays in the middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
use tray names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle cart):

LOBU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
zuja: n1 -> <LOBU tray>, n2 -> <LOBU tray>, n3 -> <LOBU tray>, n4 -> <LOBU tray>, n5 -> <LOBU tray>
josi: q1_1 -> <LOBU tray>, ..., q1_k -> <LOBU tray>
nasa: q1_1 -> <KUKA tray>, ... (one entry per LOBU tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
