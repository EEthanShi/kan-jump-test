You are completing the design of a small toy sorting line with 3 stations.

The stations are: GIPU, GUKA, NUFU. Toys move GIPU -> GUKA -> NUFU.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "jano" carries each GIPU slot to a GUKA slot.
- "supa" is an internal reshuffle of GUKA: it carries each GUKA slot to a GUKA slot.
- "gobo" carries each GUKA slot to a NUFU slot.

The line's operating rules. These must hold exactly, slot by slot:
R1.1 Running "jano" and then "supa" lands on the same slot as running "jano" alone.
     ("supa" leaves every slot that "jano" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "supa" and then "gobo" gives the same result as running "gobo" alone.
     ("gobo" cannot tell whether "supa" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "supa" twice in a row leaves every GUKA slot exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- GIPU has exactly 2 slots, called n1, n2.
- NUFU has exactly 2 slots, called s1, s2.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the line sends n1 to s1, n2 to s1.

Requirements for the middle station (GUKA):
D1.1 GUKA must be a finite set with at most 4 slots.
D1.2 "supa" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one GUKA slot).
D1.3 "jano" must carry different GIPU slots to different GUKA slots.
D1.4 Every NUFU slot must be reached by "gobo" from at least one GUKA slot.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle station.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 slots in the middle station
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every slot and every table entry;
use slot names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle station):

GUKA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
jano: n1 -> <GUKA slot>, n2 -> <GUKA slot>
supa: q1_1 -> <GUKA slot>, ..., q1_k -> <GUKA slot>
gobo: q1_1 -> <NUFU slot>, ... (one entry per GUKA slot)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
