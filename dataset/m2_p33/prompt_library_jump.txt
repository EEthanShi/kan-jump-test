You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: NOLI, SAFI, VUTO, KIRA. Books move NOLI -> SAFI -> VUTO -> KIRA.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "vopo" carries each NOLI tray to a SAFI tray.
- "boja" is an internal rearrangement of SAFI: it carries each SAFI tray to a SAFI tray.
- "tigo" carries each SAFI tray to a VUTO tray.
- "ziki" is an internal rearrangement of VUTO: it carries each VUTO tray to a VUTO tray.
- "kigo" carries each VUTO tray to a KIRA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "vopo" and then "boja" lands on the same tray as running "vopo" alone.
     ("boja" leaves every tray that "vopo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "boja" and then "tigo" gives the same result as running "tigo" alone.
     ("tigo" cannot tell whether "boja" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "boja" three times in a row leaves every SAFI tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "tigo" and then "ziki" lands on the same tray as running "tigo" alone.
     ("ziki" leaves every tray that "tigo" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "ziki" and then "kigo" gives the same result as running "kigo" alone.
     ("kigo" cannot tell whether "ziki" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "ziki" three times in a row leaves every VUTO tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- NOLI has exactly one tray, called n1.
- KIRA has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (SAFI, VUTO):
D1.1 SAFI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "boja" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one SAFI tray).
D2.1 VUTO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "ziki" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one VUTO tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

SAFI = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
vopo: n1 -> <SAFI tray>
boja: q1_1 -> <SAFI tray>, ..., q1_k -> <SAFI tray>
VUTO = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
tigo: q1_1 -> <VUTO tray>, ... (one entry per SAFI tray)
ziki: q2_1 -> <VUTO tray>, ..., q2_k -> <VUTO tray>
kigo: q2_1 -> <KIRA tray>, ... (one entry per VUTO tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
