You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: FADU, JODU, BUFU, MAZA. Books move FADU -> JODU -> BUFU -> MAZA.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "dajo" carries each FADU tray to a JODU tray.
- "fafi" is an internal rearrangement of JODU: it carries each JODU tray to a JODU tray.
- "zini" carries each JODU tray to a BUFU tray.
- "zomi" is an internal rearrangement of BUFU: it carries each BUFU tray to a BUFU tray.
- "subo" carries each BUFU tray to a MAZA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "dajo" and then "fafi" lands on the same tray as running "dajo" alone.
     ("fafi" leaves every tray that "dajo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "fafi" and then "zini" gives the same result as running "zini" alone.
     ("zini" cannot tell whether "fafi" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "fafi" twice in a row leaves every JODU tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "zini" and then "zomi" lands on the same tray as running "zini" alone.
     ("zomi" leaves every tray that "zini" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "zomi" and then "subo" gives the same result as running "subo" alone.
     ("subo" cannot tell whether "zomi" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "zomi" twice in a row leaves every BUFU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- FADU has exactly one tray, called n1.
- MAZA has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (JODU, BUFU):
D1.1 JODU must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D1.2 "fafi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one JODU tray).
D2.1 BUFU must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D2.2 "zomi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one BUFU tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

JODU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
dajo: n1 -> <JODU tray>
fafi: q1_1 -> <JODU tray>, ..., q1_k -> <JODU tray>
BUFU = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
zini: q1_1 -> <BUFU tray>, ... (one entry per JODU tray)
zomi: q2_1 -> <BUFU tray>, ..., q2_k -> <BUFU tray>
subo: q2_1 -> <MAZA tray>, ... (one entry per BUFU tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
