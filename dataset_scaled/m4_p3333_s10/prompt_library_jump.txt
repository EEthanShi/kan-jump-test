You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 6 carts.

The carts are: SUPO, POTO, NORO, JOBA, FADI, LIKO. Books move SUPO -> POTO -> NORO -> JOBA -> FADI -> LIKO.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "fafa" carries each SUPO tray to a POTO tray.
- "nani" is an internal rearrangement of POTO: it carries each POTO tray to a POTO tray.
- "dapa" carries each POTO tray to a NORO tray.
- "mavu" is an internal rearrangement of NORO: it carries each NORO tray to a NORO tray.
- "badu" carries each NORO tray to a JOBA tray.
- "guta" is an internal rearrangement of JOBA: it carries each JOBA tray to a JOBA tray.
- "podu" carries each JOBA tray to a FADI tray.
- "solu" is an internal rearrangement of FADI: it carries each FADI tray to a FADI tray.
- "buro" carries each FADI tray to a LIKO tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "fafa" and then "nani" lands on the same tray as running "fafa" alone.
     ("nani" leaves every tray that "fafa" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "nani" and then "dapa" gives the same result as running "dapa" alone.
     ("dapa" cannot tell whether "nani" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "nani" three times in a row leaves every POTO tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "dapa" and then "mavu" lands on the same tray as running "dapa" alone.
     ("mavu" leaves every tray that "dapa" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "mavu" and then "badu" gives the same result as running "badu" alone.
     ("badu" cannot tell whether "mavu" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "mavu" three times in a row leaves every NORO tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "badu" and then "guta" lands on the same tray as running "badu" alone.
     ("guta" leaves every tray that "badu" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "guta" and then "podu" gives the same result as running "podu" alone.
     ("podu" cannot tell whether "guta" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "guta" three times in a row leaves every JOBA tray exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "podu" and then "solu" lands on the same tray as running "podu" alone.
     ("solu" leaves every tray that "podu" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "solu" and then "buro" gives the same result as running "buro" alone.
     ("buro" cannot tell whether "solu" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "solu" three times in a row leaves every FADI tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- SUPO has exactly one tray, called n1.
- LIKO has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (POTO, NORO, JOBA, FADI):
D1.1 POTO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "nani" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one POTO tray).
D2.1 NORO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "mavu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one NORO tray).
D3.1 JOBA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D3.2 "guta" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one JOBA tray).
D4.1 FADI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D4.2 "solu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one FADI tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
