You are completing the design of a small toy sorting line with 6 stations.

The stations are: BOFU, VOMU, GIJI, ZOPI, ZAGA, MOTU. Toys move BOFU -> VOMU -> GIJI -> ZOPI -> ZAGA -> MOTU.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "zomo" carries each BOFU slot to a VOMU slot.
- "fogi" is an internal reshuffle of VOMU: it carries each VOMU slot to a VOMU slot.
- "posu" carries each VOMU slot to a GIJI slot.
- "sufa" is an internal reshuffle of GIJI: it carries each GIJI slot to a GIJI slot.
- "sugu" carries each GIJI slot to a ZOPI slot.
- "zodi" is an internal reshuffle of ZOPI: it carries each ZOPI slot to a ZOPI slot.
- "lapu" carries each ZOPI slot to a ZAGA slot.
- "rizi" is an internal reshuffle of ZAGA: it carries each ZAGA slot to a ZAGA slot.
- "tipa" carries each ZAGA slot to a MOTU slot.

The line's operating rules. These must hold exactly, slot by slot:
R1.1 Running "zomo" and then "fogi" lands on the same slot as running "zomo" alone.
     ("fogi" leaves every slot that "zomo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "fogi" and then "posu" gives the same result as running "posu" alone.
     ("posu" cannot tell whether "fogi" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "fogi" three times in a row leaves every VOMU slot exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "posu" and then "sufa" lands on the same slot as running "posu" alone.
     ("sufa" leaves every slot that "posu" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "sufa" and then "sugu" gives the same result as running "sugu" alone.
     ("sugu" cannot tell whether "sufa" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "sufa" three times in a row leaves every GIJI slot exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "sugu" and then "zodi" lands on the same slot as running "sugu" alone.
     ("zodi" leaves every slot that "sugu" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "zodi" and then "lapu" gives the same result as running "lapu" alone.
     ("lapu" cannot tell whether "zodi" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "zodi" three times in a row leaves every ZOPI slot exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "lapu" and then "rizi" lands on the same slot as running "lapu" alone.
     ("rizi" leaves every slot that "lapu" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "rizi" and then "tipa" gives the same result as running "tipa" alone.
     ("tipa" cannot tell whether "rizi" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "rizi" three times in a row leaves every ZAGA slot exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- BOFU has exactly one slot, called n1.
- MOTU has exactly one slot, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the line sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle stations (VOMU, GIJI, ZOPI, ZAGA):
D1.1 VOMU must be a finite set with at most 4 slots.
D1.2 "fogi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one VOMU slot).
D2.1 GIJI must be a finite set with at most 4 slots.
D2.2 "sufa" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one GIJI slot).
D3.1 ZOPI must be a finite set with at most 4 slots.
D3.2 "zodi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one ZOPI slot).
D4.1 ZAGA must be a finite set with at most 4 slots.
D4.2 "rizi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one ZAGA slot).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle stations.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 slots per middle station
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every slot and every table entry;
for station number i in the row, use slot names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

VOMU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
zomo: n1 -> <VOMU slot>
