You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 6 carts.

The carts are: GORU, VOJI, ZIPA, LADU, GOBI, JOPO. Books move GORU -> VOJI -> ZIPA -> LADU -> GOBI -> JOPO.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "rano" carries each GORU tray to a VOJI tray.
- "giko" is an internal rearrangement of VOJI: it carries each VOJI tray to a VOJI tray.
- "povo" carries each VOJI tray to a ZIPA tray.
- "zoko" is an internal rearrangement of ZIPA: it carries each ZIPA tray to a ZIPA tray.
- "dofa" carries each ZIPA tray to a LADU tray.
- "volo" is an internal rearrangement of LADU: it carries each LADU tray to a LADU tray.
- "dovu" carries each LADU tray to a GOBI tray.
- "kosa" is an internal rearrangement of GOBI: it carries each GOBI tray to a GOBI tray.
- "rilo" carries each GOBI tray to a JOPO tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "rano" and then "giko" lands on the same tray as running "rano" alone.
     ("giko" leaves every tray that "rano" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "giko" and then "povo" gives the same result as running "povo" alone.
     ("povo" cannot tell whether "giko" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "giko" three times in a row leaves every VOJI tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "povo" and then "zoko" lands on the same tray as running "povo" alone.
     ("zoko" leaves every tray that "povo" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "zoko" and then "dofa" gives the same result as running "dofa" alone.
     ("dofa" cannot tell whether "zoko" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "zoko" three times in a row leaves every ZIPA tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "dofa" and then "volo" lands on the same tray as running "dofa" alone.
     ("volo" leaves every tray that "dofa" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "volo" and then "dovu" gives the same result as running "dovu" alone.
     ("dovu" cannot tell whether "volo" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "volo" three times in a row leaves every LADU tray exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "dovu" and then "kosa" lands on the same tray as running "dovu" alone.
     ("kosa" leaves every tray that "dovu" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "kosa" and then "rilo" gives the same result as running "rilo" alone.
     ("rilo" cannot tell whether "kosa" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "kosa" three times in a row leaves every GOBI tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- GORU has exactly one tray, called n1.
- JOPO has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (VOJI, ZIPA, LADU, GOBI):
D1.1 VOJI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "giko" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one VOJI tray).
D2.1 ZIPA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "zoko" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one ZIPA tray).
D3.1 LADU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D3.2 "volo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one LADU tray).
D4.1 GOBI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D4.2 "kosa" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one GOBI tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
