You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 6 carts.

The carts are: MIGU, NOFA, GUKU, JUMI, GURO, FINO. Books move MIGU -> NOFA -> GUKU -> JUMI -> GURO -> FINO.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "mivu" carries each MIGU tray to a NOFA tray.
- "faba" is an internal rearrangement of NOFA: it carries each NOFA tray to a NOFA tray.
- "jimi" carries each NOFA tray to a GUKU tray.
- "vuza" is an internal rearrangement of GUKU: it carries each GUKU tray to a GUKU tray.
- "guti" carries each GUKU tray to a JUMI tray.
- "jubo" is an internal rearrangement of JUMI: it carries each JUMI tray to a JUMI tray.
- "lipu" carries each JUMI tray to a GURO tray.
- "tazi" is an internal rearrangement of GURO: it carries each GURO tray to a GURO tray.
- "nugu" carries each GURO tray to a FINO tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "mivu" and then "faba" lands on the same tray as running "mivu" alone.
     ("faba" leaves every tray that "mivu" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "faba" and then "jimi" gives the same result as running "jimi" alone.
     ("jimi" cannot tell whether "faba" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "faba" three times in a row leaves every NOFA tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "jimi" and then "vuza" lands on the same tray as running "jimi" alone.
     ("vuza" leaves every tray that "jimi" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "vuza" and then "guti" gives the same result as running "guti" alone.
     ("guti" cannot tell whether "vuza" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "vuza" three times in a row leaves every GUKU tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "guti" and then "jubo" lands on the same tray as running "guti" alone.
     ("jubo" leaves every tray that "guti" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "jubo" and then "lipu" gives the same result as running "lipu" alone.
     ("lipu" cannot tell whether "jubo" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "jubo" three times in a row leaves every JUMI tray exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "lipu" and then "tazi" lands on the same tray as running "lipu" alone.
     ("tazi" leaves every tray that "lipu" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "tazi" and then "nugu" gives the same result as running "nugu" alone.
     ("nugu" cannot tell whether "tazi" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "tazi" three times in a row leaves every GURO tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- MIGU has exactly one tray, called n1.
- FINO has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (NOFA, GUKU, JUMI, GURO):
D1.1 NOFA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "faba" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one NOFA tray).
D2.1 GUKU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "vuza" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one GUKU tray).
D3.1 JUMI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D3.2 "jubo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one JUMI tray).
D4.1 GURO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D4.2 "tazi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one GURO tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).
