You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 3 carts.

The carts are: PILI, LURI, RUDA. Books move PILI -> LURI -> RUDA.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "gifi" carries each PILI tray to a LURI tray.
- "nuva" is an internal rearrangement of LURI: it carries each LURI tray to a LURI tray.
- "muli" carries each LURI tray to a RUDA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "gifi" and then "nuva" lands on the same tray as running "gifi" alone.
     ("nuva" leaves every tray that "gifi" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "nuva" and then "muli" gives the same result as running "muli" alone.
     ("muli" cannot tell whether "nuva" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "nuva" three times in a row leaves every LURI tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- PILI has exactly one tray, called n1.
- RUDA has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle cart (LURI):
D1.1 LURI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "nuva" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one LURI tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle cart.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

LURI = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
gifi: n1 -> <LURI tray>
nuva: q1_1 -> <LURI tray>, ..., q1_k -> <LURI tray>
muli: q1_1 -> <RUDA tray>, ... (one entry per LURI tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
