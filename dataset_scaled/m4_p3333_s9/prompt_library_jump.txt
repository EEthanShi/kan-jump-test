You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 6 carts.

The carts are: BOGI, MIZA, RIZI, DUVO, GOLI, ROBO. Books move BOGI -> MIZA -> RIZI -> DUVO -> GOLI -> ROBO.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "jabu" carries each BOGI tray to a MIZA tray.
- "vaso" is an internal rearrangement of MIZA: it carries each MIZA tray to a MIZA tray.
- "zoru" carries each MIZA tray to a RIZI tray.
- "lani" is an internal rearrangement of RIZI: it carries each RIZI tray to a RIZI tray.
- "tafi" carries each RIZI tray to a DUVO tray.
- "munu" is an internal rearrangement of DUVO: it carries each DUVO tray to a DUVO tray.
- "vaju" carries each DUVO tray to a GOLI tray.
- "kojo" is an internal rearrangement of GOLI: it carries each GOLI tray to a GOLI tray.
- "paga" carries each GOLI tray to a ROBO tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "jabu" and then "vaso" lands on the same tray as running "jabu" alone.
     ("vaso" leaves every tray that "jabu" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "vaso" and then "zoru" gives the same result as running "zoru" alone.
     ("zoru" cannot tell whether "vaso" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "vaso" three times in a row leaves every MIZA tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "zoru" and then "lani" lands on the same tray as running "zoru" alone.
     ("lani" leaves every tray that "zoru" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "lani" and then "tafi" gives the same result as running "tafi" alone.
     ("tafi" cannot tell whether "lani" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "lani" three times in a row leaves every RIZI tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "tafi" and then "munu" lands on the same tray as running "tafi" alone.
     ("munu" leaves every tray that "tafi" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "munu" and then "vaju" gives the same result as running "vaju" alone.
     ("vaju" cannot tell whether "munu" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "munu" three times in a row leaves every DUVO tray exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "vaju" and then "kojo" lands on the same tray as running "vaju" alone.
     ("kojo" leaves every tray that "vaju" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "kojo" and then "paga" gives the same result as running "paga" alone.
     ("paga" cannot tell whether "kojo" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "kojo" three times in a row leaves every GOLI tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- BOGI has exactly one tray, called n1.
- ROBO has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (MIZA, RIZI, DUVO, GOLI):
D1.1 MIZA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "vaso" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one MIZA tray).
D2.1 RIZI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "lani" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one RIZI tray).
D3.1 DUVO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D3.2 "munu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one DUVO tray).
D4.1 GOLI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D4.2 "kojo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one GOLI tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.
