You are completing the design of a small toy sorting line with 6 stations.

The stations are: PUDA, MIBU, JONA, JUKI, VIRO, ZIJA. Toys move PUDA -> MIBU -> JONA -> JUKI -> VIRO -> ZIJA.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "tavo" carries each PUDA slot to a MIBU slot.
- "foju" is an internal reshuffle of MIBU: it carries each MIBU slot to a MIBU slot.
- "buri" carries each MIBU slot to a JONA slot.
- "jumi" is an internal reshuffle of JONA: it carries each JONA slot to a JONA slot.
- "sifo" carries each JONA slot to a JUKI slot.
- "fata" is an internal reshuffle of JUKI: it carries each JUKI slot to a JUKI slot.
- "diko" carries each JUKI slot to a VIRO slot.
- "fazo" is an internal reshuffle of VIRO: it carries each VIRO slot to a VIRO slot.
- "karu" carries each VIRO slot to a ZIJA slot.

The line's operating rules. These must hold exactly, slot by slot:
R1.1 Running "tavo" and then "foju" lands on the same slot as running "tavo" alone.
     ("foju" leaves every slot that "tavo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "foju" and then "buri" gives the same result as running "buri" alone.
     ("buri" cannot tell whether "foju" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "foju" three times in a row leaves every MIBU slot exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "buri" and then "jumi" lands on the same slot as running "buri" alone.
     ("jumi" leaves every slot that "buri" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "jumi" and then "sifo" gives the same result as running "sifo" alone.
     ("sifo" cannot tell whether "jumi" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "jumi" three times in a row leaves every JONA slot exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "sifo" and then "fata" lands on the same slot as running "sifo" alone.
     ("fata" leaves every slot that "sifo" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "fata" and then "diko" gives the same result as running "diko" alone.
     ("diko" cannot tell whether "fata" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "fata" three times in a row leaves every JUKI slot exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "diko" and then "fazo" lands on the same slot as running "diko" alone.
     ("fazo" leaves every slot that "diko" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "fazo" and then "karu" gives the same result as running "karu" alone.
     ("karu" cannot tell whether "fazo" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "fazo" three times in a row leaves every VIRO slot exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- PUDA has exactly one slot, called n1.
- ZIJA has exactly one slot, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the line sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle stations (MIBU, JONA, JUKI, VIRO):
D1.1 MIBU must be a finite set with at most 4 slots.
D1.2 "foju" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one MIBU slot).
D2.1 JONA must be a finite set with at most 4 slots.
D2.2 "jumi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one JONA slot).
D3.1 JUKI must be a finite set with at most 4 slots.
D3.2 "fata" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one JUKI slot).
D4.1 VIRO must be a finite set with at most 4 slots.
D4.2 "fazo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one VIRO slot).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle stations.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 slots per middle station
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every slot and every table entry;
for station number i in the row, use slot names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

MIBU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
tavo: n1 -> <MIBU slot>

VIRO = {q4_1, ..., q4_k}
