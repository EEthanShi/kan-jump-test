You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: NIFU, FUDU, BURO, JIGI. Books move NIFU -> FUDU -> BURO -> JIGI.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "kopu" carries each NIFU tray to a FUDU tray.
- "gusu" is an internal rearrangement of FUDU: it carries each FUDU tray to a FUDU tray.
- "dijo" carries each FUDU tray to a BURO tray.
- "naso" is an internal rearrangement of BURO: it carries each BURO tray to a BURO tray.
- "gara" carries each BURO tray to a JIGI tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "kopu" and then "gusu" lands on the same tray as running "kopu" alone.
     ("gusu" leaves every tray that "kopu" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "gusu" and then "dijo" gives the same result as running "dijo" alone.
     ("dijo" cannot tell whether "gusu" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "gusu" twice in a row leaves every FUDU tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "dijo" and then "naso" lands on the same tray as running "dijo" alone.
     ("naso" leaves every tray that "dijo" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "naso" and then "gara" gives the same result as running "gara" alone.
     ("gara" cannot tell whether "naso" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "naso" three times in a row leaves every BURO tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- NIFU has exactly one tray, called n1.
- JIGI has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (FUDU, BURO):
D1.1 FUDU must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D1.2 "gusu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one FUDU tray).
D2.1 BURO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "naso" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one BURO tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

FUDU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
kopu: n1 -> <FUDU tray>
gusu: q1_1 -> <FUDU tray>, ..., q1_k -> <FUDU tray>
BURO = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
dijo: q1_1 -> <BURO tray>, ... (one entry per FUDU tray)
naso: q2_1 -> <BURO tray>, ..., q2_k -> <BURO tray>
gara: q2_1 -> <JIGI tray>, ... (one entry per BURO tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
