You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 5 carts.

The carts are: JUNA, NONI, RAZO, MATA, FUPU. Books move JUNA -> NONI -> RAZO -> MATA -> FUPU.
There are 7 moving steps:
- "zani" carries each JUNA tray to a NONI tray.
- "gaju" is an internal rearrangement of NONI: it carries each NONI tray to a NONI tray.
- "lupu" carries each NONI tray to a RAZO tray.
- "noza" is an internal rearrangement of RAZO: it carries each RAZO tray to a RAZO tray.
- "davi" carries each RAZO tray to a MATA tray.
- "kuku" is an internal rearrangement of MATA: it carries each MATA tray to a MATA tray.
- "roji" carries each MATA tray to a FUPU tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "zani" and then "gaju" lands on the same tray as running "zani" alone.
     ("gaju" leaves every tray that "zani" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "gaju" and then "lupu" gives the same result as running "lupu" alone.
     ("lupu" cannot tell whether "gaju" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "gaju" three times in a row leaves every NONI tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "lupu" and then "noza" lands on the same tray as running "lupu" alone.
     ("noza" leaves every tray that "lupu" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "noza" and then "davi" gives the same result as running "davi" alone.
     ("davi" cannot tell whether "noza" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "noza" three times in a row leaves every RAZO tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "davi" and then "kuku" lands on the same tray as running "davi" alone.
     ("kuku" leaves every tray that "davi" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "kuku" and then "roji" gives the same result as running "roji" alone.
     ("roji" cannot tell whether "kuku" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "kuku" three times in a row leaves every MATA tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- JUNA has exactly one tray, called n1.
- FUPU has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (NONI, RAZO, MATA):
D1.1 NONI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "gaju" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one NONI tray).
D2.1 RAZO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "noza" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one RAZO tray).
D3.1 MATA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D3.2 "kuku" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one MATA tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

NONI = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
zani: n1 -> <NONI tray>
gaju: q1_1 -> <NONI tray>, ..., q1_k -> <NONI tray>
RAZO = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
lupu: q1_1 -> <RAZO tray>, ... (one entry per NONI tray)
noza: q2_1 -> <RAZO tray>, ..., q2_k -> <RAZO tray>
MATA = {q3_1, ..., q3_k}
davi: q2_1 -> <MATA tray>, ... (one entry per RAZO tray)
kuku: q3_1 -> <MATA tray>, ..., q3_k -> <MATA tray>
roji: q3_1 -> <FUPU tray>, ... (one entry per MATA tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
