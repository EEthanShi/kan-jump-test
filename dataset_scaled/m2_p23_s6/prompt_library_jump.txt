You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: GAKU, DANA, DIKI, DUJI. Books move GAKU -> DANA -> DIKI -> DUJI.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "robi" carries each GAKU tray to a DANA tray.
- "guzu" is an internal rearrangement of DANA: it carries each DANA tray to a DANA tray.
- "fiji" carries each DANA tray to a DIKI tray.
- "piko" is an internal rearrangement of DIKI: it carries each DIKI tray to a DIKI tray.
- "rofo" carries each DIKI tray to a DUJI tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "robi" and then "guzu" lands on the same tray as running "robi" alone.
     ("guzu" leaves every tray that "robi" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "guzu" and then "fiji" gives the same result as running "fiji" alone.
     ("fiji" cannot tell whether "guzu" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "guzu" twice in a row leaves every DANA tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "fiji" and then "piko" lands on the same tray as running "fiji" alone.
     ("piko" leaves every tray that "fiji" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "piko" and then "rofo" gives the same result as running "rofo" alone.
     ("rofo" cannot tell whether "piko" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "piko" three times in a row leaves every DIKI tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- GAKU has exactly one tray, called n1.
- DUJI has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (DANA, DIKI):
D1.1 DANA must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D1.2 "guzu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one DANA tray).
D2.1 DIKI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "piko" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one DIKI tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

DANA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
robi: n1 -> <DANA tray>
guzu: q1_1 -> <DANA tray>, ..., q1_k -> <DANA tray>
DIKI = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
fiji: q1_1 -> <DIKI tray>, ... (one entry per DANA tray)
piko: q2_1 -> <DIKI tray>, ..., q2_k -> <DIKI tray>
rofo: q2_1 -> <DUJI tray>, ... (one entry per DIKI tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
