You are completing the design of a small toy sorting line with 3 stations.

The stations are: DIBO, ZIJI, NASO. Toys move DIBO -> ZIJI -> NASO.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "bijo" carries each DIBO slot to a ZIJI slot.
- "kufa" is an internal reshuffle of ZIJI: it carries each ZIJI slot to a ZIJI slot.
- "joma" carries each ZIJI slot to a NASO slot.

The line's operating rules. These must hold exactly, slot by slot:
R1.1 Running "bijo" and then "kufa" lands on the same slot as running "bijo" alone.
     ("kufa" leaves every slot that "bijo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "kufa" and then "joma" gives the same result as running "joma" alone.
     ("joma" cannot tell whether "kufa" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "kufa" twice in a row leaves every ZIJI slot exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- DIBO has exactly 2 slots, called n1, n2.
- NASO has exactly 1 slot, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the line sends n1 to s1, n2 to s1.

Requirements for the middle station (ZIJI):
D1.1 ZIJI must be a finite set with at most 1 slot.
D1.2 List the "kufa" table entries in slot order q1_1, q1_2, ..., one entry per slot.
D1.3 List the "bijo" table entries in DIBO slot order n1, n2.
D1.4 Every NASO slot must be reached by "joma" from at least one ZIJI slot.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle station.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 slots in the middle station
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every slot and every table entry;
use slot names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle station):

ZIJI = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
bijo: n1 -> <ZIJI slot>, n2 -> <ZIJI slot>
kufa: q1_1 -> <ZIJI slot>, ..., q1_k -> <ZIJI slot>
joma: q1_1 -> <NASO slot>, ... (one entry per ZIJI slot)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
