You are completing the design of a small toy sorting line with 4 stations.

The stations are: DOTU, GOJA, TARO, VOSI. Toys move DOTU -> GOJA -> TARO -> VOSI.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "jaba" carries each DOTU slot to a GOJA slot.
- "rabo" is an internal reshuffle of GOJA: it carries each GOJA slot to a GOJA slot.
- "doki" carries each GOJA slot to a TARO slot.
- "bogu" is an internal reshuffle of TARO: it carries each TARO slot to a TARO slot.
- "nuti" carries each TARO slot to a VOSI slot.

The line's operating rules. These must hold exactly, slot by slot:
R1.1 Running "jaba" and then "rabo" lands on the same slot as running "jaba" alone.
     ("rabo" leaves every slot that "jaba" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "rabo" and then "doki" gives the same result as running "doki" alone.
     ("doki" cannot tell whether "rabo" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "rabo" three times in a row leaves every GOJA slot exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "doki" and then "bogu" lands on the same slot as running "doki" alone.
     ("bogu" leaves every slot that "doki" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "bogu" and then "nuti" gives the same result as running "nuti" alone.
     ("nuti" cannot tell whether "bogu" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "bogu" twice in a row leaves every TARO slot exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- DOTU has exactly one slot, called n1.
- VOSI has exactly one slot, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the line sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle stations (GOJA, TARO):
D1.1 GOJA must be a finite set with at most 4 slots.
D1.2 "rabo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one GOJA slot).
D2.1 TARO must be a finite set with at most 3 slots.
D2.2 "bogu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one TARO slot).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle stations.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 slots per middle station
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every slot and every table entry;
for station number i in the row, use slot names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

GOJA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
jaba: n1 -> <GOJA slot>
rabo: q1_1 -> <GOJA slot>, ..., q1_k -> <GOJA slot>
TARO = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
doki: q1_1 -> <TARO slot>, ... (one entry per GOJA slot)
bogu: q2_1 -> <TARO slot>, ..., q2_k -> <TARO slot>
nuti: q2_1 -> <VOSI slot>, ... (one entry per TARO slot)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
