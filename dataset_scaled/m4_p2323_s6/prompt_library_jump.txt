You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 6 carts.

The carts are: RUZA, SIJU, DIZI, JIZO, VODU, BABO. Books move RUZA -> SIJU -> DIZI -> JIZO -> VODU -> BABO.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "nizi" carries each RUZA tray to a SIJU tray.
- "pifo" is an internal rearrangement of SIJU: it carries each SIJU tray to a SIJU tray.
- "rura" carries each SIJU tray to a DIZI tray.
- "vili" is an internal rearrangement of DIZI: it carries each DIZI tray to a DIZI tray.
- "tado" carries each DIZI tray to a JIZO tray.
- "kapo" is an internal rearrangement of JIZO: it carries each JIZO tray to a JIZO tray.
- "lido" carries each JIZO tray to a VODU tray.
- "folo" is an internal rearrangement of VODU: it carries each VODU tray to a VODU tray.
- "gosi" carries each VODU tray to a BABO tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "nizi" and then "pifo" lands on the same tray as running "nizi" alone.
     ("pifo" leaves every tray that "nizi" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "pifo" and then "rura" gives the same result as running "rura" alone.
     ("rura" cannot tell whether "pifo" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "pifo" twice in a row leaves every SIJU tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "rura" and then "vili" lands on the same tray as running "rura" alone.
     ("vili" leaves every tray that "rura" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "vili" and then "tado" gives the same result as running "tado" alone.
     ("tado" cannot tell whether "vili" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "vili" three times in a row leaves every DIZI tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "tado" and then "kapo" lands on the same tray as running "tado" alone.
     ("kapo" leaves every tray that "tado" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "kapo" and then "lido" gives the same result as running "lido" alone.
     ("lido" cannot tell whether "kapo" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "kapo" twice in a row leaves every JIZO tray exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "lido" and then "folo" lands on the same tray as running "lido" alone.
     ("folo" leaves every tray that "lido" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "folo" and then "gosi" gives the same result as running "gosi" alone.
     ("gosi" cannot tell whether "folo" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "folo" three times in a row leaves every VODU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- RUZA has exactly one tray, called n1.
- BABO has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (SIJU, DIZI, JIZO, VODU):
D1.1 SIJU must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D1.2 "pifo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one SIJU tray).
D2.1 DIZI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "vili" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one DIZI tray).
D3.1 JIZO must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D3.2 "kapo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one JIZO tray).
D4.1 VODU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D4.2 "folo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one VODU tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
