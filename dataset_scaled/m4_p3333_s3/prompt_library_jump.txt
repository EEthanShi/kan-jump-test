You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 6 carts.

The carts are: MUDA, DUMI, FOFI, MOBA, SABO, JUPA. Books move MUDA -> DUMI -> FOFI -> MOBA -> SABO -> JUPA.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "nuju" carries each MUDA tray to a DUMI tray.
- "difi" is an internal rearrangement of DUMI: it carries each DUMI tray to a DUMI tray.
- "mafu" carries each DUMI tray to a FOFI tray.
- "fiko" is an internal rearrangement of FOFI: it carries each FOFI tray to a FOFI tray.
- "gaju" carries each FOFI tray to a MOBA tray.
- "panu" is an internal rearrangement of MOBA: it carries each MOBA tray to a MOBA tray.
- "dana" carries each MOBA tray to a SABO tray.
- "limu" is an internal rearrangement of SABO: it carries each SABO tray to a SABO tray.
- "vizu" carries each SABO tray to a JUPA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "nuju" and then "difi" lands on the same tray as running "nuju" alone.
     ("difi" leaves every tray that "nuju" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "difi" and then "mafu" gives the same result as running "mafu" alone.
     ("mafu" cannot tell whether "difi" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "difi" three times in a row leaves every DUMI tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "mafu" and then "fiko" lands on the same tray as running "mafu" alone.
     ("fiko" leaves every tray that "mafu" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "fiko" and then "gaju" gives the same result as running "gaju" alone.
     ("gaju" cannot tell whether "fiko" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "fiko" three times in a row leaves every FOFI tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "gaju" and then "panu" lands on the same tray as running "gaju" alone.
     ("panu" leaves every tray that "gaju" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "panu" and then "dana" gives the same result as running "dana" alone.
     ("dana" cannot tell whether "panu" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "panu" three times in a row leaves every MOBA tray exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "dana" and then "limu" lands on the same tray as running "dana" alone.
     ("limu" leaves every tray that "dana" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "limu" and then "vizu" gives the same result as running "vizu" alone.
     ("vizu" cannot tell whether "limu" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "limu" three times in a row leaves every SABO tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- MUDA has exactly one tray, called n1.
- JUPA has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (DUMI, FOFI, MOBA, SABO):
D1.1 DUMI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "difi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one DUMI tray).
D2.1 FOFI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "fiko" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one FOFI tray).
D3.1 MOBA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D3.2 "panu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one MOBA tray).
D4.1 SABO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D4.2 "limu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one SABO tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).
