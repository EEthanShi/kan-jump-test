You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 6 carts.

The carts are: TAKI, VAMI, FUGO, MAGO, SUGO, TOBU. Books move TAKI -> VAMI -> FUGO -> MAGO -> SUGO -> TOBU.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "fufa" carries each TAKI tray to a VAMI tray.
- "rati" is an internal rearrangement of VAMI: it carries each VAMI tray to a VAMI tray.
- "sosu" carries each VAMI tray to a FUGO tray.
- "kidi" is an internal rearrangement of FUGO: it carries each FUGO tray to a FUGO tray.
- "loki" carries each FUGO tray to a MAGO tray.
- "gafi" is an internal rearrangement of MAGO: it carries each MAGO tray to a MAGO tray.
- "tuka" carries each MAGO tray to a SUGO tray.
- "nusi" is an internal rearrangement of SUGO: it carries each SUGO tray to a SUGO tray.
- "biba" carries each SUGO tray to a TOBU tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "fufa" and then "rati" lands on the same tray as running "fufa" alone.
     ("rati" leaves every tray that "fufa" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "rati" and then "sosu" gives the same result as running "sosu" alone.
     ("sosu" cannot tell whether "rati" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "rati" twice in a row leaves every VAMI tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "sosu" and then "kidi" lands on the same tray as running "sosu" alone.
     ("kidi" leaves every tray that "sosu" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "kidi" and then "loki" gives the same result as running "loki" alone.
     ("loki" cannot tell whether "kidi" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "kidi" twice in a row leaves every FUGO tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "loki" and then "gafi" lands on the same tray as running "loki" alone.
     ("gafi" leaves every tray that "loki" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "gafi" and then "tuka" gives the same result as running "tuka" alone.
     ("tuka" cannot tell whether "gafi" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "gafi" twice in a row leaves every MAGO tray exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "tuka" and then "nusi" lands on the same tray as running "tuka" alone.
     ("nusi" leaves every tray that "tuka" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "nusi" and then "biba" gives the same result as running "biba" alone.
     ("biba" cannot tell whether "nusi" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "nusi" twice in a row leaves every SUGO tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- TAKI has exactly one tray, called n1.
- TOBU has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (VAMI, FUGO, MAGO, SUGO):
D1.1 VAMI must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D1.2 "rati" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one VAMI tray).
D2.1 FUGO must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D2.2 "kidi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one FUGO tray).
D3.1 MAGO must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D3.2 "gafi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one MAGO tray).
D4.1 SUGO must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D4.2 "nusi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one SUGO tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
