You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: RUSU, FUTI, SIFU, DAVA. Books move RUSU -> FUTI -> SIFU -> DAVA.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "foda" carries each RUSU tray to a FUTI tray.
- "vamu" is an internal rearrangement of FUTI: it carries each FUTI tray to a FUTI tray.
- "fila" carries each FUTI tray to a SIFU tray.
- "nama" is an internal rearrangement of SIFU: it carries each SIFU tray to a SIFU tray.
- "daja" carries each SIFU tray to a DAVA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "foda" and then "vamu" lands on the same tray as running "foda" alone.
     ("vamu" leaves every tray that "foda" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "vamu" and then "fila" gives the same result as running "fila" alone.
     ("fila" cannot tell whether "vamu" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "vamu" three times in a row leaves every FUTI tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "fila" and then "nama" lands on the same tray as running "fila" alone.
     ("nama" leaves every tray that "fila" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "nama" and then "daja" gives the same result as running "daja" alone.
     ("daja" cannot tell whether "nama" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "nama" three times in a row leaves every SIFU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- RUSU has exactly one tray, called n1.
- DAVA has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (FUTI, SIFU):
D1.1 FUTI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "vamu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one FUTI tray).
D2.1 SIFU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "nama" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one SIFU tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

FUTI = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
foda: n1 -> <FUTI tray>
vamu: q1_1 -> <FUTI tray>, ..., q1_k -> <FUTI tray>
SIFU = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
fila: q1_1 -> <SIFU tray>, ... (one entry per FUTI tray)
nama: q2_1 -> <SIFU tray>, ..., q2_k -> <SIFU tray>
daja: q2_1 -> <DAVA tray>, ... (one entry per SIFU tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
