You are completing the design of a small toy sorting line with 3 stations.

The stations are: JUSI, VURA, TUKA. Toys move JUSI -> VURA -> TUKA.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "zubi" carries each JUSI slot to a VURA slot.
- "diba" is an internal reshuffle of VURA: it carries each VURA slot to a VURA slot.
- "boja" carries each VURA slot to a TUKA slot.

The line's operating rules. These must hold exactly, slot by slot:
R1.1 Running "zubi" and then "diba" lands on the same slot as running "zubi" alone.
     ("diba" leaves every slot that "zubi" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "diba" and then "boja" gives the same result as running "boja" alone.
     ("boja" cannot tell whether "diba" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "diba" twice in a row leaves every VURA slot exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- JUSI has exactly 2 slots, called n1, n2.
- TUKA has exactly 1 slot, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the line sends n1 to s1, n2 to s1.

Requirements for the middle station (VURA):
D1.1 VURA must be a finite set with at most 4 slots.
D1.2 "diba" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one VURA slot).
D1.3 "zubi" must carry different JUSI slots to different VURA slots.
D1.4 Every TUKA slot must be reached by "boja" from at least one VURA slot.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle station.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 slots in the middle station
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every slot and every table entry;
use slot names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle station):

VURA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
zubi: n1 -> <VURA slot>, n2 -> <VURA slot>
diba: q1_1 -> <VURA slot>, ..., q1_k -> <VURA slot>
boja: q1_1 -> <TUKA slot>, ... (one entry per VURA slot)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
