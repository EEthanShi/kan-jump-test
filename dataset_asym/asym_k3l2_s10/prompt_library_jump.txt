You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 3 carts.

The carts are: FUTO, RAGU, RAZA. Books move FUTO -> RAGU -> RAZA.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "naru" carries each FUTO tray to a RAGU tray.
- "faru" is an internal rearrangement of RAGU: it carries each RAGU tray to a RAGU tray.
- "jodo" carries each RAGU tray to a RAZA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "naru" and then "faru" lands on the same tray as running "naru" alone.
     ("faru" leaves every tray that "naru" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "faru" and then "jodo" gives the same result as running "jodo" alone.
     ("jodo" cannot tell whether "faru" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "faru" twice in a row leaves every RAGU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- FUTO has exactly 3 trays, called n1, n2, n3.
- RAZA has exactly 2 trays, called s1, s2.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1, n2 to s1, n3 to s1.

Requirements for the middle cart (RAGU):
D1.1 RAGU must be a finite set with at most 5 trays.
D1.2 "faru" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one RAGU tray).
D1.3 "naru" must carry different FUTO trays to different RAGU trays.
D1.4 Every RAZA tray must be reached by "jodo" from at least one RAGU tray.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle cart.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays in the middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
use tray names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle cart):

RAGU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
naru: n1 -> <RAGU tray>, n2 -> <RAGU tray>, n3 -> <RAGU tray>
faru: q1_1 -> <RAGU tray>, ..., q1_k -> <RAGU tray>
jodo: q1_1 -> <RAZA tray>, ... (one entry per RAGU tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
