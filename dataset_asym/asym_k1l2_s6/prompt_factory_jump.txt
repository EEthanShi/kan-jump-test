You are completing the design of a small toy sorting line with 3 stations.

The stations are: JOZI, ZIJO, JIRA. Toys move JOZI -> ZIJO -> JIRA.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "fato" carries each JOZI slot to a ZIJO slot.
- "lafa" is an internal reshuffle of ZIJO: it carries each ZIJO slot to a ZIJO slot.
- "fira" carries each ZIJO slot to a JIRA slot.

The line's operating rules. These must hold exactly, slot by slot:
R1.1 Running "fato" and then "lafa" lands on the same slot as running "fato" alone.
     ("lafa" leaves every slot that "fato" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "lafa" and then "fira" gives the same result as running "fira" alone.
     ("fira" cannot tell whether "lafa" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "lafa" twice in a row leaves every ZIJO slot exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- JOZI has exactly 1 slot, called n1.
- JIRA has exactly 2 slots, called s1, s2.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the line sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle station (ZIJO):
D1.1 ZIJO must be a finite set with at most 3 slots.
D1.2 "lafa" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one ZIJO slot).
D1.3 "fato" must carry different JOZI slots to different ZIJO slots.
D1.4 Every JIRA slot must be reached by "fira" from at least one ZIJO slot.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle station.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 3 slots in the middle station
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every slot and every table entry;
use slot names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle station):

ZIJO = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
fato: n1 -> <ZIJO slot>
lafa: q1_1 -> <ZIJO slot>, ..., q1_k -> <ZIJO slot>
fira: q1_1 -> <JIRA slot>, ... (one entry per ZIJO slot)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
