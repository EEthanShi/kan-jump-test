You are completing the design of a small toy sorting line with 3 stations.

The stations are: BIFI, PUDA, DOVO. Toys move BIFI -> PUDA -> DOVO.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "bini" carries each BIFI slot to a PUDA slot.
- "jusa" is an internal reshuffle of PUDA: it carries each PUDA slot to a PUDA slot.
- "tima" carries each PUDA slot to a DOVO slot.

The line's operating rules. These must hold exactly, slot by slot:
R1.1 Running "bini" and then "jusa" lands on the same slot as running "bini" alone.
     ("jusa" leaves every slot that "bini" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "jusa" and then "tima" gives the same result as running "tima" alone.
     ("tima" cannot tell whether "jusa" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "jusa" twice in a row leaves every PUDA slot exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- BIFI has exactly 4 slots, called n1, n2, n3, n4.
- DOVO has exactly 1 slot, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the line sends n1 to s1, n2 to s1, n3 to s1, n4 to s1.

Requirements for the middle station (PUDA):
D1.1 PUDA must be a finite set with at most 6 slots.
D1.2 "jusa" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one PUDA slot).
D1.3 "bini" must carry different BIFI slots to different PUDA slots.
D1.4 Every DOVO slot must be reached by "tima" from at least one PUDA slot.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle station.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 6 slots in the middle station
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every slot and every table entry;
use slot names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle station):

PUDA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
bini: n1 -> <PUDA slot>, n2 -> <PUDA slot>, n3 -> <PUDA slot>, n4 -> <PUDA slot>
jusa: q1_1 -> <PUDA slot>, ..., q1_k -> <PUDA slot>
tima: q1_1 -> <DOVO slot>, ... (one entry per PUDA slot)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
