You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: RABU, RUMU, LUKU, ZINA. Books move RABU -> RUMU -> LUKU -> ZINA.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "mulu" carries each RABU tray to a RUMU tray.
- "gifa" is an internal rearrangement of RUMU: it carries each RUMU tray to a RUMU tray.
- "koza" carries each RUMU tray to a LUKU tray.
- "jara" is an internal rearrangement of LUKU: it carries each LUKU tray to a LUKU tray.
- "laki" carries each LUKU tray to a ZINA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "mulu" and then "gifa" lands on the same tray as running "mulu" alone.
     ("gifa" leaves every tray that "mulu" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "gifa" and then "koza" gives the same result as running "koza" alone.
     ("koza" cannot tell whether "gifa" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "gifa" three times in a row leaves every RUMU tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "koza" and then "jara" lands on the same tray as running "koza" alone.
     ("jara" leaves every tray that "koza" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "jara" and then "laki" gives the same result as running "laki" alone.
     ("laki" cannot tell whether "jara" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "jara" twice in a row leaves every LUKU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- RABU has exactly one tray, called n1.
- ZINA has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (RUMU, LUKU):
D1.1 RUMU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "gifa" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one RUMU tray).
D2.1 LUKU must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D2.2 "jara" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one LUKU tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

RUMU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
mulu: n1 -> <RUMU tray>
gifa: q1_1 -> <RUMU tray>, ..., q1_k -> <RUMU tray>
LUKU = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
koza: q1_1 -> <LUKU tray>, ... (one entry per RUMU tray)
jara: q2_1 -> <LUKU tray>, ..., q2_k -> <LUKU tray>
laki: q2_1 -> <ZINA tray>, ... (one entry per LUKU tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
