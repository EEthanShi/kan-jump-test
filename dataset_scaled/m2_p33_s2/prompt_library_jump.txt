You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: RIJA, KUJA, KALO, KOGU. Books move RIJA -> KUJA -> KALO -> KOGU.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "gobo" carries each RIJA tray to a KUJA tray.
- "gipi" is an internal rearrangement of KUJA: it carries each KUJA tray to a KUJA tray.
- "lufi" carries each KUJA tray to a KALO tray.
- "faso" is an internal rearrangement of KALO: it carries each KALO tray to a KALO tray.
- "gitu" carries each KALO tray to a KOGU tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "gobo" and then "gipi" lands on the same tray as running "gobo" alone.
     ("gipi" leaves every tray that "gobo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "gipi" and then "lufi" gives the same result as running "lufi" alone.
     ("lufi" cannot tell whether "gipi" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "gipi" three times in a row leaves every KUJA tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "lufi" and then "faso" lands on the same tray as running "lufi" alone.
     ("faso" leaves every tray that "lufi" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "faso" and then "gitu" gives the same result as running "gitu" alone.
     ("gitu" cannot tell whether "faso" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "faso" three times in a row leaves every KALO tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- RIJA has exactly one tray, called n1.
- KOGU has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (KUJA, KALO):
D1.1 KUJA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "gipi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one KUJA tray).
D2.1 KALO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "faso" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one KALO tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

KUJA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
gobo: n1 -> <KUJA tray>
gipi: q1_1 -> <KUJA tray>, ..., q1_k -> <KUJA tray>
KALO = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
lufi: q1_1 -> <KALO tray>, ... (one entry per KUJA tray)
faso: q2_1 -> <KALO tray>, ..., q2_k -> <KALO tray>
gitu: q2_1 -> <KOGU tray>, ... (one entry per KALO tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
