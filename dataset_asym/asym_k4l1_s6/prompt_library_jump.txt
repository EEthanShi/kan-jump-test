You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 3 carts.

The carts are: SOTA, MUVA, LIVO. Books move SOTA -> MUVA -> LIVO.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "zovo" carries each SOTA tray to a MUVA tray.
- "vama" is an internal rearrangement of MUVA: it carries each MUVA tray to a MUVA tray.
- "biba" carries each MUVA tray to a LIVO tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "zovo" and then "vama" lands on the same tray as running "zovo" alone.
     ("vama" leaves every tray that "zovo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "vama" and then "biba" gives the same result as running "biba" alone.
     ("biba" cannot tell whether "vama" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "vama" twice in a row leaves every MUVA tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- SOTA has exactly 4 trays, called n1, n2, n3, n4.
- LIVO has exactly 1 tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1, n2 to s1, n3 to s1, n4 to s1.

Requirements for the middle cart (MUVA):
D1.1 MUVA must be a finite set with at most 6 trays.
D1.2 "vama" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one MUVA tray).
D1.3 "zovo" must carry different SOTA trays to different MUVA trays.
D1.4 Every LIVO tray must be reached by "biba" from at least one MUVA tray.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle cart.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 6 trays in the middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
use tray names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle cart):

MUVA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
zovo: n1 -> <MUVA tray>, n2 -> <MUVA tray>, n3 -> <MUVA tray>, n4 -> <MUVA tray>
vama: q1_1 -> <MUVA tray>, ..., q1_k -> <MUVA tray>
biba: q1_1 -> <LIVO tray>, ... (one entry per MUVA tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
