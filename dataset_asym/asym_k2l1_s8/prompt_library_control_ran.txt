You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 3 carts.

The carts are: DARA, DAPA, JUJO. Books move DARA -> DAPA -> JUJO.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "filu" carries each DARA tray to a DAPA tray.
- "sodo" is an internal rearrangement of DAPA: it carries each DAPA tray to a DAPA tray.
- "soju" carries each DAPA tray to a JUJO tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "filu" and then "sodo" lands on the same tray as running "filu" alone.
     ("sodo" leaves every tray that "filu" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "sodo" and then "soju" gives the same result as running "soju" alone.
     ("soju" cannot tell whether "sodo" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "sodo" twice in a row leaves every DAPA tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- DARA has exactly 2 trays, called n1, n2.
- JUJO has exactly 1 tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1, n2 to s1.

Requirements for the middle cart (DAPA):
D1.1 DAPA must be a finite set with at most 1 tray.
D1.2 List the "sodo" table entries in tray order q1_1, q1_2, ..., one entry per tray.
D1.3 List the "filu" table entries in DARA tray order n1, n2.
D1.4 Every JUJO tray must be reached by "soju" from at least one DAPA tray.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle cart.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 trays in the middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
use tray names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle cart):

DAPA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
filu: n1 -> <DAPA tray>, n2 -> <DAPA tray>
sodo: q1_1 -> <DAPA tray>, ..., q1_k -> <DAPA tray>
soju: q1_1 -> <JUJO tray>, ... (one entry per DAPA tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
