You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: ZOPO, BINU, GARI, GOTI. Books move ZOPO -> BINU -> GARI -> GOTI.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "dubo" carries each ZOPO tray to a BINU tray.
- "pifa" is an internal rearrangement of BINU: it carries each BINU tray to a BINU tray.
- "tozu" carries each BINU tray to a GARI tray.
- "romi" is an internal rearrangement of GARI: it carries each GARI tray to a GARI tray.
- "zovo" carries each GARI tray to a GOTI tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "dubo" and then "pifa" lands on the same tray as running "dubo" alone.
     ("pifa" leaves every tray that "dubo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "pifa" and then "tozu" gives the same result as running "tozu" alone.
     ("tozu" cannot tell whether "pifa" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "pifa" three times in a row leaves every BINU tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "tozu" and then "romi" lands on the same tray as running "tozu" alone.
     ("romi" leaves every tray that "tozu" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "romi" and then "zovo" gives the same result as running "zovo" alone.
     ("zovo" cannot tell whether "romi" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "romi" three times in a row leaves every GARI tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- ZOPO has exactly one tray, called n1.
- GOTI has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (BINU, GARI):
D1.1 BINU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "pifa" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one BINU tray).
D2.1 GARI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "romi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one GARI tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

BINU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
dubo: n1 -> <BINU tray>
pifa: q1_1 -> <BINU tray>, ..., q1_k -> <BINU tray>
GARI = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
tozu: q1_1 -> <GARI tray>, ... (one entry per BINU tray)
romi: q2_1 -> <GARI tray>, ..., q2_k -> <GARI tray>
zovo: q2_1 -> <GOTI tray>, ... (one entry per GARI tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
