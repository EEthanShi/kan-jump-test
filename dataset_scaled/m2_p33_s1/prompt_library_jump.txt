You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: ZUTO, ZUDI, NARO, MIMU. Books move ZUTO -> ZUDI -> NARO -> MIMU.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "gida" carries each ZUTO tray to a ZUDI tray.
- "mijo" is an internal rearrangement of ZUDI: it carries each ZUDI tray to a ZUDI tray.
- "juso" carries each ZUDI tray to a NARO tray.
- "jita" is an internal rearrangement of NARO: it carries each NARO tray to a NARO tray.
- "puji" carries each NARO tray to a MIMU tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "gida" and then "mijo" lands on the same tray as running "gida" alone.
     ("mijo" leaves every tray that "gida" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "mijo" and then "juso" gives the same result as running "juso" alone.
     ("juso" cannot tell whether "mijo" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "mijo" three times in a row leaves every ZUDI tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "juso" and then "jita" lands on the same tray as running "juso" alone.
     ("jita" leaves every tray that "juso" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "jita" and then "puji" gives the same result as running "puji" alone.
     ("puji" cannot tell whether "jita" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "jita" three times in a row leaves every NARO tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- ZUTO has exactly one tray, called n1.
- MIMU has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (ZUDI, NARO):
D1.1 ZUDI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "mijo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one ZUDI tray).
D2.1 NARO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "jita" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one NARO tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

ZUDI = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
gida: n1 -> <ZUDI tray>
mijo: q1_1 -> <ZUDI tray>, ..., q1_k -> <ZUDI tray>
NARO = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
juso: q1_1 -> <NARO tray>, ... (one entry per ZUDI tray)
jita: q2_1 -> <NARO tray>, ..., q2_k -> <NARO tray>
puji: q2_1 -> <MIMU tray>, ... (one entry per NARO tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
