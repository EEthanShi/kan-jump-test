You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: SUPA, ZOJI, SIZA, BOKI. Books move SUPA -> ZOJI -> SIZA -> BOKI.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "puni" carries each SUPA tray to a ZOJI tray.
- "rilu" is an internal rearrangement of ZOJI: it carries each ZOJI tray to a ZOJI tray.
- "suli" carries each ZOJI tray to a SIZA tray.
- "tiro" is an internal rearrangement of SIZA: it carries each SIZA tray to a SIZA tray.
- "pana" carries each SIZA tray to a BOKI tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "puni" and then "rilu" lands on the same tray as running "puni" alone.
     ("rilu" leaves every tray that "puni" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "rilu" and then "suli" gives the same result as running "suli" alone.
     ("suli" cannot tell whether "rilu" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "rilu" three times in a row leaves every ZOJI tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "suli" and then "tiro" lands on the same tray as running "suli" alone.
     ("tiro" leaves every tray that "suli" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "tiro" and then "pana" gives the same result as running "pana" alone.
     ("pana" cannot tell whether "tiro" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "tiro" three times in a row leaves every SIZA tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- SUPA has exactly one tray, called n1.
- BOKI has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (ZOJI, SIZA):
D1.1 ZOJI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "rilu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one ZOJI tray).
D2.1 SIZA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "tiro" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one SIZA tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

ZOJI = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
puni: n1 -> <ZOJI tray>
rilu: q1_1 -> <ZOJI tray>, ..., q1_k -> <ZOJI tray>
SIZA = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
suli: q1_1 -> <SIZA tray>, ... (one entry per ZOJI tray)
tiro: q2_1 -> <SIZA tray>, ..., q2_k -> <SIZA tray>
pana: q2_1 -> <BOKI tray>, ... (one entry per SIZA tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
