You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: PIPO, ZIJA, GOTO, KOGO. Books move PIPO -> ZIJA -> GOTO -> KOGO.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "fili" carries each PIPO tray to a ZIJA tray.
- "rapa" is an internal rearrangement of ZIJA: it carries each ZIJA tray to a ZIJA tray.
- "fata" carries each ZIJA tray to a GOTO tray.
- "luvi" is an internal rearrangement of GOTO: it carries each GOTO tray to a GOTO tray.
- "komi" carries each GOTO tray to a KOGO tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "fili" and then "rapa" lands on the same tray as running "fili" alone.
     ("rapa" leaves every tray that "fili" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "rapa" and then "fata" gives the same result as running "fata" alone.
     ("fata" cannot tell whether "rapa" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "rapa" three times in a row leaves every ZIJA tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "fata" and then "luvi" lands on the same tray as running "fata" alone.
     ("luvi" leaves every tray that "fata" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "luvi" and then "komi" gives the same result as running "komi" alone.
     ("komi" cannot tell whether "luvi" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "luvi" twice in a row leaves every GOTO tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- PIPO has exactly one tray, called n1.
- KOGO has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (ZIJA, GOTO):
D1.1 ZIJA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "rapa" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one ZIJA tray).
D2.1 GOTO must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D2.2 "luvi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one GOTO tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

ZIJA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
fili: n1 -> <ZIJA tray>
rapa: q1_1 -> <ZIJA tray>, ..., q1_k -> <ZIJA tray>
GOTO = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
fata: q1_1 -> <GOTO tray>, ... (one entry per ZIJA tray)
luvi: q2_1 -> <GOTO tray>, ..., q2_k -> <GOTO tray>
komi: q2_1 -> <KOGO tray>, ... (one entry per GOTO tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
