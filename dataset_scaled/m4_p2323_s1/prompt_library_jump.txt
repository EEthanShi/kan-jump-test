You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 6 carts.

The carts are: PILI, KAGU, ZIVU, KIJO, JIZA, SODA. Books move PILI -> KAGU -> ZIVU -> KIJO -> JIZA -> SODA.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "juvo" carries each PILI tray to a KAGU tray.
- "baja" is an internal rearrangement of KAGU: it carries each KAGU tray to a KAGU tray.
- "vibi" carries each KAGU tray to a ZIVU tray.
- "kudo" is an internal rearrangement of ZIVU: it carries each ZIVU tray to a ZIVU tray.
- "mami" carries each ZIVU tray to a KIJO tray.
- "dola" is an internal rearrangement of KIJO: it carries each KIJO tray to a KIJO tray.
- "mujo" carries each KIJO tray to a JIZA tray.
- "jovi" is an internal rearrangement of JIZA: it carries each JIZA tray to a JIZA tray.
- "pigi" carries each JIZA tray to a SODA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "juvo" and then "baja" lands on the same tray as running "juvo" alone.
     ("baja" leaves every tray that "juvo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "baja" and then "vibi" gives the same result as running "vibi" alone.
     ("vibi" cannot tell whether "baja" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "baja" twice in a row leaves every KAGU tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "vibi" and then "kudo" lands on the same tray as running "vibi" alone.
     ("kudo" leaves every tray that "vibi" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "kudo" and then "mami" gives the same result as running "mami" alone.
     ("mami" cannot tell whether "kudo" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "kudo" three times in a row leaves every ZIVU tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "mami" and then "dola" lands on the same tray as running "mami" alone.
     ("dola" leaves every tray that "mami" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "dola" and then "mujo" gives the same result as running "mujo" alone.
     ("mujo" cannot tell whether "dola" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "dola" twice in a row leaves every KIJO tray exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "mujo" and then "jovi" lands on the same tray as running "mujo" alone.
     ("jovi" leaves every tray that "mujo" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "jovi" and then "pigi" gives the same result as running "pigi" alone.
     ("pigi" cannot tell whether "jovi" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "jovi" three times in a row leaves every JIZA tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- PILI has exactly one tray, called n1.
- SODA has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (KAGU, ZIVU, KIJO, JIZA):
D1.1 KAGU must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D1.2 "baja" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one KAGU tray).
D2.1 ZIVU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "kudo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one ZIVU tray).
D3.1 KIJO must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D3.2 "dola" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one KIJO tray).
D4.1 JIZA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D4.2 "jovi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one JIZA tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
