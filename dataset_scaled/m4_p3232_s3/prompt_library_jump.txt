You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 6 carts.

The carts are: JIZU, VOMO, NATA, LARA, MIZU, GILA. Books move JIZU -> VOMO -> NATA -> LARA -> MIZU -> GILA.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "nura" carries each JIZU tray to a VOMO tray.
- "gapi" is an internal rearrangement of VOMO: it carries each VOMO tray to a VOMO tray.
- "ruru" carries each VOMO tray to a NATA tray.
- "zona" is an internal rearrangement of NATA: it carries each NATA tray to a NATA tray.
- "palu" carries each NATA tray to a LARA tray.
- "ragu" is an internal rearrangement of LARA: it carries each LARA tray to a LARA tray.
- "nupa" carries each LARA tray to a MIZU tray.
- "guna" is an internal rearrangement of MIZU: it carries each MIZU tray to a MIZU tray.
- "ribu" carries each MIZU tray to a GILA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "nura" and then "gapi" lands on the same tray as running "nura" alone.
     ("gapi" leaves every tray that "nura" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "gapi" and then "ruru" gives the same result as running "ruru" alone.
     ("ruru" cannot tell whether "gapi" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "gapi" three times in a row leaves every VOMO tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "ruru" and then "zona" lands on the same tray as running "ruru" alone.
     ("zona" leaves every tray that "ruru" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "zona" and then "palu" gives the same result as running "palu" alone.
     ("palu" cannot tell whether "zona" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "zona" twice in a row leaves every NATA tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "palu" and then "ragu" lands on the same tray as running "palu" alone.
     ("ragu" leaves every tray that "palu" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "ragu" and then "nupa" gives the same result as running "nupa" alone.
     ("nupa" cannot tell whether "ragu" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "ragu" three times in a row leaves every LARA tray exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "nupa" and then "guna" lands on the same tray as running "nupa" alone.
     ("guna" leaves every tray that "nupa" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "guna" and then "ribu" gives the same result as running "ribu" alone.
     ("ribu" cannot tell whether "guna" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "guna" twice in a row leaves every MIZU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- JIZU has exactly one tray, called n1.
- GILA has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (VOMO, NATA, LARA, MIZU):
D1.1 VOMO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "gapi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one VOMO tray).
D2.1 NATA must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D2.2 "zona" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one NATA tray).
D3.1 LARA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D3.2 "ragu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one LARA tray).
D4.1 MIZU must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D4.2 "guna" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one MIZU tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
