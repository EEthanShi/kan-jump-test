You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: DORI, RUKA, KUGI, NOSU. Books move DORI -> RUKA -> KUGI -> NOSU.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "jimo" carries each DORI tray to a RUKA tray.
- "zoka" is an internal rearrangement of RUKA: it carries each RUKA tray to a RUKA tray.
- "gula" carries each RUKA tray to a KUGI tray.
- "supi" is an internal rearrangement of KUGI: it carries each KUGI tray to a KUGI tray.
- "koka" carries each KUGI tray to a NOSU tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "jimo" and then "zoka" lands on the same tray as running "jimo" alone.
     ("zoka" leaves every tray that "jimo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "zoka" and then "gula" gives the same result as running "gula" alone.
     ("gula" cannot tell whether "zoka" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "zoka" three times in a row leaves every RUKA tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "gula" and then "supi" lands on the same tray as running "gula" alone.
     ("supi" leaves every tray that "gula" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "supi" and then "koka" gives the same result as running "koka" alone.
     ("koka" cannot tell whether "supi" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "supi" three times in a row leaves every KUGI tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- DORI has exactly one tray, called n1.
- NOSU has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (RUKA, KUGI):
D1.1 RUKA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "zoka" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one RUKA tray).
D2.1 KUGI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "supi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one KUGI tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

RUKA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
jimo: n1 -> <RUKA tray>
zoka: q1_1 -> <RUKA tray>, ..., q1_k -> <RUKA tray>
KUGI = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
gula: q1_1 -> <KUGI tray>, ... (one entry per RUKA tray)
supi: q2_1 -> <KUGI tray>, ..., q2_k -> <KUGI tray>
koka: q2_1 -> <NOSU tray>, ... (one entry per KUGI tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
