You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: ZIVO, DUGA, LIJO, TUPI. Books move ZIVO -> DUGA -> LIJO -> TUPI.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "sodi" carries each ZIVO tray to a DUGA tray.
- "baji" is an internal rearrangement of DUGA: it carries each DUGA tray to a DUGA tray.
- "ruta" carries each DUGA tray to a LIJO tray.
- "ruda" is an internal rearrangement of LIJO: it carries each LIJO tray to a LIJO tray.
- "gilu" carries each LIJO tray to a TUPI tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "sodi" and then "baji" lands on the same tray as running "sodi" alone.
     ("baji" leaves every tray that "sodi" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "baji" and then "ruta" gives the same result as running "ruta" alone.
     ("ruta" cannot tell whether "baji" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "baji" three times in a row leaves every DUGA tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "ruta" and then "ruda" lands on the same tray as running "ruta" alone.
     ("ruda" leaves every tray that "ruta" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "ruda" and then "gilu" gives the same result as running "gilu" alone.
     ("gilu" cannot tell whether "ruda" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "ruda" twice in a row leaves every LIJO tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- ZIVO has exactly one tray, called n1.
- TUPI has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (DUGA, LIJO):
D1.1 DUGA must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "baji" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one DUGA tray).
D2.1 LIJO must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D2.2 "ruda" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one LIJO tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

DUGA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
sodi: n1 -> <DUGA tray>
baji: q1_1 -> <DUGA tray>, ..., q1_k -> <DUGA tray>
LIJO = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
ruta: q1_1 -> <LIJO tray>, ... (one entry per DUGA tray)
ruda: q2_1 -> <LIJO tray>, ..., q2_k -> <LIJO tray>
gilu: q2_1 -> <TUPI tray>, ... (one entry per LIJO tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
