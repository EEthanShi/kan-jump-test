You are completing the design of a small toy sorting line with 3 stations.

The stations are: MUSI, NOMU, PUFU. Toys move MUSI -> NOMU -> PUFU.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "babo" carries each MUSI slot to a NOMU slot.
- "zovu" is an internal reshuffle of NOMU: it carries each NOMU slot to a NOMU slot.
- "busi" carries each NOMU slot to a PUFU slot.

The line's operating rules. These must hold exactly, slot by slot:
R1.1 Running "babo" and then "zovu" lands on the same slot as running "babo" alone.
     ("zovu" leaves every slot that "babo" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "zovu" and then "busi" gives the same result as running "busi" alone.
     ("busi" cannot tell whether "zovu" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "zovu" twice in a row leaves every NOMU slot exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- MUSI has exactly 3 slots, called n1, n2, n3.
- PUFU has exactly 2 slots, called s1, s2.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the line sends n1 to s1, n2 to s1, n3 to s1.

Requirements for the middle station (NOMU):
D1.1 NOMU must be a finite set with at most 5 slots.
D1.2 "zovu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one NOMU slot).
D1.3 "babo" must carry different MUSI slots to different NOMU slots.
D1.4 Every PUFU slot must be reached by "busi" from at least one NOMU slot.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle station.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 slots in the middle station
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every slot and every table entry;
use slot names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle station):

NOMU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
babo: n1 -> <NOMU slot>, n2 -> <NOMU slot>, n3 -> <NOMU slot>
zovu: q1_1 -> <NOMU slot>, ..., q1_k -> <NOMU slot>
busi: q1_1 -> <PUFU slot>, ... (one entry per NOMU slot)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
