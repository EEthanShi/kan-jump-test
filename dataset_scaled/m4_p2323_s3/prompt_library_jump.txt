You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 6 carts.

The carts are: VARI, MUSA, GANU, GONO, SIRU, NANI. Books move VARI -> MUSA -> GANU -> GONO -> SIRU -> NANI.
There are 9 moving steps:
- "bona" carries each VARI tray to a MUSA tray.
- "roga" is an internal rearrangement of MUSA: it carries each MUSA tray to a MUSA tray.
- "zumu" carries each MUSA tray to a GANU tray.
- "fugu" is an internal rearrangement of GANU: it carries each GANU tray to a GANU tray.
- "kobu" carries each GANU tray to a GONO tray.
- "nara" is an internal rearrangement of GONO: it carries each GONO tray to a GONO tray.
- "divi" carries each GONO tray to a SIRU tray.
- "faki" is an internal rearrangement of SIRU: it carries each SIRU tray to a SIRU tray.
- "zaku" carries each SIRU tray to a NANI tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "bona" and then "roga" lands on the same tray as running "bona" alone.
     ("roga" leaves every tray that "bona" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "roga" and then "zumu" gives the same result as running "zumu" alone.
     ("zumu" cannot tell whether "roga" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "roga" twice in a row leaves every MUSA tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "zumu" and then "fugu" lands on the same tray as running "zumu" alone.
     ("fugu" leaves every tray that "zumu" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "fugu" and then "kobu" gives the same result as running "kobu" alone.
     ("kobu" cannot tell whether "fugu" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "fugu" three times in a row leaves every GANU tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "kobu" and then "nara" lands on the same tray as running "kobu" alone.
     ("nara" leaves every tray that "kobu" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "nara" and then "divi" gives the same result as running "divi" alone.
     ("divi" cannot tell whether "nara" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "nara" twice in a row leaves every GONO tray exactly where it started.
R4.1 Running "divi" and then "faki" lands on the same tray as running "divi" alone.
     ("faki" leaves every tray that "divi" produces unchanged.)
R4.2 Running "faki" and then "zaku" gives the same result as running "zaku" alone.
     ("zaku" cannot tell whether "faki" was applied first.)
R4.3 Running "faki" three times in a row leaves every SIRU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- VARI has exactly one tray, called n1.
- NANI has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (MUSA, GANU, GONO, SIRU):
D1.1 MUSA must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D1.2 "roga" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one MUSA tray).
D2.1 GANU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "fugu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one GANU tray).
D3.1 GONO must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D3.2 "nara" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one GONO tray).
D4.1 SIRU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D4.2 "faki" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one SIRU tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

MUSA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
bona: n1 -> <MUSA tray>
