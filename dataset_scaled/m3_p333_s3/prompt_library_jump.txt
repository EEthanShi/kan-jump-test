You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 5 carts.

The carts are: ZANI, SAVU, BOLO, LALI, LONA. Books move ZANI -> SAVU -> BOLO -> LALI -> LONA.
There are 7 moving steps:
- "biki" carries each ZANI tray to a SAVU tray.
- "zipi" is an internal rearrangement of SAVU: it carries each SAVU tray to a SAVU tray.
- "kigu" carries each SAVU tray to a BOLO tray.
- "zasu" is an internal rearrangement of BOLO: it carries each BOLO tray to a BOLO tray.
- "runu" carries each BOLO tray to a LALI tray.
- "lujo" is an internal rearrangement of LALI: it carries each LALI tray to a LALI tray.
- "misu" carries each LALI tray to a LONA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "biki" and then "zipi" lands on the same tray as running "biki" alone.
     ("zipi" leaves every tray that "biki" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "zipi" and then "kigu" gives the same result as running "kigu" alone.
     ("kigu" cannot tell whether "zipi" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "zipi" three times in a row leaves every SAVU tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "kigu" and then "zasu" lands on the same tray as running "kigu" alone.
     ("zasu" leaves every tray that "kigu" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "zasu" and then "runu" gives the same result as running "runu" alone.
     ("runu" cannot tell whether "zasu" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "zasu" three times in a row leaves every BOLO tray exactly where it started.
R3.1 Running "runu" and then "lujo" lands on the same tray as running "runu" alone.
     ("lujo" leaves every tray that "runu" produces unchanged.)
R3.2 Running "lujo" and then "misu" gives the same result as running "misu" alone.
     ("misu" cannot tell whether "lujo" was applied first.)
R3.3 Running "lujo" three times in a row leaves every LALI tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- ZANI has exactly one tray, called n1.
- LONA has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (SAVU, BOLO, LALI):
D1.1 SAVU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "zipi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one SAVU tray).
D2.1 BOLO must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "zasu" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one BOLO tray).
D3.1 LALI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D3.2 "lujo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one LALI tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

SAVU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
biki: n1 -> <SAVU tray>
zipi: q1_1 -> <SAVU tray>, ..., q1_k -> <SAVU tray>
BOLO = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
kigu: q1_1 -> <BOLO tray>, ... (one entry per SAVU tray)
zasu: q2_1 -> <BOLO tray>, ..., q2_k -> <BOLO tray>
LALI = {q3_1, ..., q3_k}
runu: q2_1 -> <LALI tray>, ... (one entry per BOLO tray)
lujo: q3_1 -> <LALI tray>, ..., q3_k -> <LALI tray>
misu: q3_1 -> <LONA tray>, ... (one entry per LALI tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
