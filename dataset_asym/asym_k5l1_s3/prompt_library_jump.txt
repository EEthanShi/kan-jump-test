You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 3 carts.

The carts are: ZUBA, KUFA, KADI. Books move ZUBA -> KUFA -> KADI.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "gila" carries each ZUBA tray to a KUFA tray.
- "fuju" is an internal rearrangement of KUFA: it carries each KUFA tray to a KUFA tray.
- "gora" carries each KUFA tray to a KADI tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "gila" and then "fuju" lands on the same tray as running "gila" alone.
     ("fuju" leaves every tray that "gila" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "fuju" and then "gora" gives the same result as running "gora" alone.
     ("gora" cannot tell whether "fuju" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "fuju" twice in a row leaves every KUFA tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- ZUBA has exactly 5 trays, called n1, n2, n3, n4, n5.
- KADI has exactly 1 tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1, n2 to s1, n3 to s1, n4 to s1, n5 to s1.

Requirements for the middle cart (KUFA):
D1.1 KUFA must be a finite set with at most 7 trays.
D1.2 "fuju" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one KUFA tray).
D1.3 "gila" must carry different ZUBA trays to different KUFA trays.
D1.4 Every KADI tray must be reached by "gora" from at least one KUFA tray.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle cart.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 7 trays in the middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
use tray names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle cart):

KUFA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
gila: n1 -> <KUFA tray>, n2 -> <KUFA tray>, n3 -> <KUFA tray>, n4 -> <KUFA tray>, n5 -> <KUFA tray>
fuju: q1_1 -> <KUFA tray>, ..., q1_k -> <KUFA tray>
gora: q1_1 -> <KADI tray>, ... (one entry per KUFA tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
