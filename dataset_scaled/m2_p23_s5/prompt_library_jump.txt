You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: MAKU, KIMA, PUPI, BUPI. Books move MAKU -> KIMA -> PUPI -> BUPI.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "sasi" carries each MAKU tray to a KIMA tray.
- "fada" is an internal rearrangement of KIMA: it carries each KIMA tray to a KIMA tray.
- "fugi" carries each KIMA tray to a PUPI tray.
- "palo" is an internal rearrangement of PUPI: it carries each PUPI tray to a PUPI tray.
- "jufa" carries each PUPI tray to a BUPI tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "sasi" and then "fada" lands on the same tray as running "sasi" alone.
     ("fada" leaves every tray that "sasi" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "fada" and then "fugi" gives the same result as running "fugi" alone.
     ("fugi" cannot tell whether "fada" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "fada" twice in a row leaves every KIMA tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "fugi" and then "palo" lands on the same tray as running "fugi" alone.
     ("palo" leaves every tray that "fugi" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "palo" and then "jufa" gives the same result as running "jufa" alone.
     ("jufa" cannot tell whether "palo" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "palo" three times in a row leaves every PUPI tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- MAKU has exactly one tray, called n1.
- BUPI has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (KIMA, PUPI):
D1.1 KIMA must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D1.2 "fada" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one KIMA tray).
D2.1 PUPI must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "palo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one PUPI tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

KIMA = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
sasi: n1 -> <KIMA tray>
fada: q1_1 -> <KIMA tray>, ..., q1_k -> <KIMA tray>
PUPI = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
fugi: q1_1 -> <PUPI tray>, ... (one entry per KIMA tray)
palo: q2_1 -> <PUPI tray>, ..., q2_k -> <PUPI tray>
jufa: q2_1 -> <BUPI tray>, ... (one entry per PUPI tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
