You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 3 carts.

The carts are: TINO, TIDU, BIKU. Books move TINO -> TIDU -> BIKU.
There are 3 moving steps:
- "nunu" carries each TINO tray to a TIDU tray.
- "domi" is an internal rearrangement of TIDU: it carries each TIDU tray to a TIDU tray.
- "zalo" carries each TIDU tray to a BIKU tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "nunu" and then "domi" lands on the same tray as running "nunu" alone.
     ("domi" leaves every tray that "nunu" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "domi" and then "zalo" gives the same result as running "zalo" alone.
     ("zalo" cannot tell whether "domi" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "domi" twice in a row leaves every TIDU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- TINO has exactly 2 trays, called n1, n2.
- BIKU has exactly 1 tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1, n2 to s1.

Requirements for the middle cart (TIDU):
D1.1 TIDU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D1.2 "domi" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one TIDU tray).
D1.3 "nunu" must carry different TINO trays to different TIDU trays.
D1.4 Every BIKU tray must be reached by "zalo" from at least one TIDU tray.

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle cart.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 4 trays in the middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
use tray names q1_1, q1_2, ... for the middle cart):

TIDU = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
nunu: n1 -> <TIDU tray>, n2 -> <TIDU tray>
domi: q1_1 -> <TIDU tray>, ..., q1_k -> <TIDU tray>
zalo: q1_1 -> <BIKU tray>, ... (one entry per TIDU tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
