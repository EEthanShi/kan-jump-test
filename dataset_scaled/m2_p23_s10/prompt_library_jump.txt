You are completing the design of a small book sorting row of carts with 4 carts.

The carts are: JOMA, MINO, NUNU, JIZA. Books move JOMA -> MINO -> NUNU -> JIZA.
There are 5 moving steps:
- "vivi" carries each JOMA tray to a MINO tray.
- "gili" is an internal rearrangement of MINO: it carries each MINO tray to a MINO tray.
- "dugu" carries each MINO tray to a NUNU tray.
- "milo" is an internal rearrangement of NUNU: it carries each NUNU tray to a NUNU tray.
- "lobi" carries each NUNU tray to a JIZA tray.

The row's operating rules. These must hold exactly, tray by tray:
R1.1 Running "vivi" and then "gili" lands on the same tray as running "vivi" alone.
     ("gili" leaves every tray that "vivi" produces unchanged.)
R1.2 Running "gili" and then "dugu" gives the same result as running "dugu" alone.
     ("dugu" cannot tell whether "gili" was applied first.)
R1.3 Running "gili" twice in a row leaves every MINO tray exactly where it started.
R2.1 Running "dugu" and then "milo" lands on the same tray as running "dugu" alone.
     ("milo" leaves every tray that "dugu" produces unchanged.)
R2.2 Running "milo" and then "lobi" gives the same result as running "lobi" alone.
     ("lobi" cannot tell whether "milo" was applied first.)
R2.3 Running "milo" three times in a row leaves every NUNU tray exactly where it started.

Recorded observations (fixed; your design must reproduce them exactly):
- JOMA has exactly one tray, called n1.
- JIZA has exactly one tray, called s1.
- The observed end-to-end behavior of the row sends n1 to s1.

Requirements for the middle carts (MINO, NUNU):
D1.1 MINO must be a finite set with at most 3 trays.
D1.2 "gili" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one MINO tray).
D2.1 NUNU must be a finite set with at most 4 trays.
D2.2 "milo" must NOT be the do-nothing move (it must move at least one NUNU tray).

Your task: give one complete concrete design of the middle carts.

Answer format bound: designs with up to 5 trays per middle cart
are well-formed answers (the requirements above may restrict this further).

Answer in exactly this format (listing every tray and every table entry;
for cart number i in the row, use tray names qi_1, qi_2, ...):

MINO = {q1_1, ..., q1_k}
vivi: n1 -> <MINO tray>
gili: q1_1 -> <MINO tray>, ..., q1_k -> <MINO tray>
NUNU = {q2_1, ..., q2_k}
dugu: q1_1 -> <NUNU tray>, ... (one entry per MINO tray)
milo: q2_1 -> <NUNU tray>, ..., q2_k -> <NUNU tray>
lobi: q2_1 -> <JIZA tray>, ... (one entry per NUNU tray)

Every rule and requirement above must hold exactly for your tables. Do not
explain; output only the lines in the format above.
